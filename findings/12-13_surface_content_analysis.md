# 12–13 - Surface Content Analysis

## Executive Summary

Tab 2’s positive relationship between long-view rate and future sessions does **not appear to be explained by basic content composition or historical content quality**.

### Key Findings

* All major surfaces contain approximately **99.6% normal videos**.
* Tab 2 serves slightly longer and newer content, but its upload-type and tag distributions are broadly similar to Tab 1.
* Historical content quality is also comparable:

  * Tab 1 historical long-play rate: **36.4%**
  * Tab 2: **37.4%**
  * Tab 4: **40.4%**
* Tab 4 has the highest historical long-play rate yet still shows a negative long-view/future-session relationship.

### Conclusion

Content quality or taxonomy alone does not explain Tab 2’s behavior.

The next hypothesis is that the relationship is driven by the **user × product-surface experience**. A within-user analysis will test whether the Tab 2 effect remains among the same users who interact with both Tabs 1 and 2.
